How the techniques behave
  • Size exclusion (Sephadex). The number is the exclusion limit. Proteins larger than the limit are excluded from the beads and elute early in the void; smaller ones enter the pores and are slowed. Each run gives just these two outcomes, so proteins that share an outcome stay mixed.
  • Ion exchange (DEAE) is an anion exchanger: it binds proteins that are negatively charged at the buffer pH (pI < pH); positive or neutral proteins flow through. Charge is negative when pH > pI.
  • SDS-PAGE reports subunit mass (one band per polypeptide). Native gel (pH 8.8) shows charge: negative proteins run to the anode (+), positive to the cathode (−). UV-Vis gives A410/A280, which flags coloured haem proteins.
